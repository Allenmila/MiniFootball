Established in 2018, Built for Glory

MAG was established and incorporated under the Companies Act in 2018. Since its inception, the organization has achieved significant success in the minifootball arena. MAG is proudly affiliated with the Africa Minifootball Confederation (AMC) and the World Minifootball Federation (WMF).
Since inception has rapidly position Ghana as a competitive force in African and global minifootball through structured participation in the elite continental and global minifootball competitions.
The team has participated in several prestigious tournaments including 4 Africa Minifootball Confederation (AMC) Cup championships and 3 World Minifootball Federation (WMF) World Cup championships. MAG is also recognized by the International University Sports Federation (FISU).

Partner With Mini Football Ghana

Join us in shaping the future of minifootball in Ghana. From grassroots development to international competitions, our partners play a key role in driving growth, empowering talent, and elevating the game on the global stage. Our ambition is matched only by the talent of our players, yet we face critical hurdles that stand in the way of national pride. Currently, limited financial resources often prevent us from securing consistent, high-quality training grounds and, more crucially, funding international travel. The most painful reality is that some of our most elite and essential players are forced to stay behind during major tournaments simply because we cannot afford their tickets for passage. We are seeking dedicated sponsors and partners to help us fix this. Your support ensures that no world-class Ghanaian talent is left on the sidelines.
